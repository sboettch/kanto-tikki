## 1. Methodology & Acoustic Classification Framework

Rather than applying broad, generic noise warnings across the entire transit corridor, HomeTikki evaluates noise through an **empirical micro-geography framework** benchmarked against field-verified urban soundscapes. Noise in Tokyo and Kanagawa is categorized into distinct acoustic archetypes based on frequency profiles, time windows, and spatial propagation.

### Acoustic Archetype Taxonomy

| Archetype Code | Label (EN / JA) | Primary Frequency & Mechanism | Typical Time Window | Primary Propagation & Mitigation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`yaki_imo`** | Mobile Megaphones / 巡回スピーカー | Mid-frequency vocal looping (`800 Hz – 2.5 kHz`), slow mobile source (`<15 km/h`) | Autumn/Winter 17:00–20:00 | Bounces through low-rise residential grids; cultural fixture rather than distress. |
| **`gaisensha`** | Political Sound Vans / 政治街宣車 | High-decibel industrial PA horns (`100–115 dB` at source), directional megaphone arrays | Weekends 11:00–17:00 | Confined to major multi-lane avenues and civic gathering points; requires double glazing on front-facing units. |
| **`cult_religious`** | Religious Loudspeakers / 宗教街宣勧誘 | Portable PA loudspeakers and high-traffic pedestrian concourse tract distribution | Evenings & Weekends | Concentrated at station rotaries and interchange plazas; attenuates within 1 block into residential grid. |
| **`nomikai_rowdy`** | Late-Night Izakaya Noise / 繁華街・泥酔騒音 | Human conversational bursts, shouting, glass recycling crate handling (`85–95 dB`) | Nightly 21:00–04:30 (Thurs–Sat peak) | Heavy along narrow pedestrian dining alleys (*yokochō*); buffers rapidly with 100m setback. |
| **`tourism_spikes`** | Tourist Luggage Rumbles / 観光スーツケース音 | Hard polyurethane wheel impact over textured tiles and stone pavers (`150–500 Hz` vibration) | Mornings 06:00–09:00 & Checkouts | Affects hotel access lanes and traditional stone-paved streets; back lanes remain silent. |
| **`daikoku_mod`** | Daikoku Tuner Meets / 大黒PA改造車排気音 | Low-frequency sub-bass exhaust pulses (`40–120 Hz`), high-rpm acceleration | Friday & Saturday 23:00–03:00 | Highway flyovers and industrial bypasses; low frequencies penetrate standard glazing, requiring T-3/T-4 acoustic windows. |
| **`bosozoku`** | Motorcycle Convoys / 旧車會・爆音バイク | Rhythmically modulated straight-pipe exhaust notes (`80–105 dB`), group convoys | Weekend Nights 00:00–03:30 | Multi-lane straightaways (Route 15 / Dai-Ichi Keihin, Route 1); 1 block of urban fabric provides ~18 dB attenuation. |
| **`boat_diesel`** | Marine Engine Idles / 漁船・遊漁船ディーゼル | Low-frequency continuous marine diesel engine thrum (`200–600 rpm` idle) | Dawn 04:30–06:15 | Confined to waterfront canal basins and boat slips; tranquil natural backdrop for setback units. |
| **`train_viaduct`** | Railway Viaduct Rumble / 高架鉄道通過音 | Steel-wheel-on-rail vibration and concrete viaduct resonance | 05:00–00:30 | Immediate viaduct frontage; southern and canal-facing units experience acoustic shadows. |

## 2. Institutional & Open Data Citations

All noise watch benchmarks are grounded in public safety logs, administrative open data, and institutional reporting:

1. **Kanagawa Prefectural Police Headquarters (神奈川県警)**:
   - *Bōsōzoku Countermeasures Dedicated Desk*: Tel `045-211-1212` (`police.pref.kanagawa.jp`). Documented monitoring logs for the Bayshore Expressway (Wangan Route), Route 357, Route 132, and Daikoku Futo Parking Area access roads.
2. **Tokyo Metropolitan Police Department (警視庁)**:
   - *Public Safety and General Counseling Desk*: `#9110` / Precinct Blotters for Atago (Daimon/Shimbashi), Mita (Diplomatic Row), Shinagawa (Kitashinagawa/Route 15), and Kamata (Station Plaza/Bourbon Road).
   - *Demonstration & March Route Registry*: Permitted civic assembly and sound van tracking around Hibiya Park, Ginza Showa-dori, and Shiba Park.
3. **Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism (MLIT / 国土交通省)**:
   - *Keihin River Office (京浜河川事務所)*: River levee ambient monitoring and Tama river bridge acoustic propagation studies (Rokugō Bridge / Route 15).
   - *Annual Railway Congestion and Noise Standards (鉄道騒音規制基準)*: Viaduct noise attenuation metrics along the Tokaido trunk lines and Keikyu main line.
4. **Ward Administrative & Environmental Divisions**:
   - *Chūō Ward Environmental Health Section (中央区環境衛生課)*: Living environment guidelines and tourism pedestrian impact assessments for Tsukiji, Ginza East, and Ningyōchō.
   - *Minato Ward Environmental Noise Open Data (港区環境騒音調査)*: Nighttime decibel monitoring across Shimbashi Karasumori, Roppongi, and Mita.
   - *Ōta Ward Environmental Noise Monitoring Map (大田区生活環境保全条例)*: Shitamachi residential acoustic surveys (Umeyashiki, Kamata, Rokugōdote).
   - *Kawasaki Ward Town Planning & Crime Prevention Division (川崎区地域振興課)*: Station East Exit plaza public address ordinances and entertainment district monitoring.
   - *Yokohama City Environmental Planning Bureau (横浜市環境創造局)*: Portside, Minato Mirai 21 acoustic zoning, and Nishi Ward entertainment noise filings.
5. **Commercial Registries & Guild Disclosures**:
   - *Street Kart Operations*: Published concession routes and vehicle registrations for Central Tokyo (Harumi-dori/Roppongi loops) and Yokohama (Minato Mirai loop).
   - *Local Merchant & Neighborhood Associations*: Amazake Yokochō Merchant Board, Tsukiji Outer Market Association, Shinagawa Shukuba-machi Historical Preservation Society, Bourbon Road Izakaya Union, Koyasu Fishery Cooperative Association, and Noge Food & Beverage Association.

## 5. Architectural Mitigation Standards & Field Rules

When evaluating individual rental units within these acoustic environments, HomeTikki applies standard Japanese architectural insulation standards (**JIS A 4706**):

- **Grade T-1 (-25 dB attenuation)**: Standard single-pane aluminum sash. Acceptable for quiet shitamachi back-alleys (e.g., Umeyashiki, Shimbamba west grid, Koyasu canal edge).
- **Grade T-2 (-30 dB attenuation)**: Standard double-glazed (pair glass) or heavy single-glazed unit. Recommended for commercial shopping arcades and station pedestrian concourses.
- **Grade T-3 (-35 dB attenuation)**: High-performance double-glazed acoustic sash or secondary interior sash (*uchimado* / 内窓). Required for arterial frontage along Route 15 (Dai-Ichi Keihin) and elevated highway corridors in Yokohama/Tsurumi.
- **Grade T-4 (-40 dB attenuation)**: Specialized double-window acoustic envelope. Recommended for direct viaduct frontage or active entertainment alleyway frontage (Kamata East, Noge, Shimbashi Karasumori).
